Tension & Dread

An exploration in atmospheric fiction.

do not turn back

The Vessel

A tale told through the eyes of a single soul, bound to endure whatever the protagonist endures. No escape is offered here, only dread that gathers slowly, like fog settling in a hollow. Every footstep is counted. Every silence is measured. The wanderer does not run. To run would be to admit fear, and fear is a door that opens only inward.

The Triggers

Borrowing the slow pacing and shadowed spaces of old games and film, the writing withholds more than it reveals. What stays hidden grows louder than anything shown. The reader's own imagination fills the dark between the words, and imagination is the most treacherous narrator of all.

The Atmosphere

Monotone. Nihilistic. Emotionally detached. Rooted in bleakness and gothic dread, stripped of polish until only raw tension remains. There is no catharsis and no release, only the slow recognition that the tale was never about escaping, but about what the wanderer has already become.

The Relic

Something lingers after the tale ends. A residue, like cold ash in the hearth long after the fire has died. The unease that follows you out of the room is not fear of what happened, but suspicion that it never finished happening. The relic is no ghost and no monster. It is the certainty that you were being watched while you read.
